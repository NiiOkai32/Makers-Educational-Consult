Practical checklists from Makers Education Consult to help you prepare for a productive conversation.

CHECKLIST / 01

Prepare for a project or thesis consultation

  • Write down your topic and the question you want to answer.
  • Gather your department’s brief, marking criteria and citation requirements.
  • Bring a draft or outline and note where you feel stuck.
  • List the data you have and how it was collected. Remove personal identifiers before sharing.
  • Set out your deadline and the milestones before it.

CHECKLIST / 02

Prepare your CV

  • Choose the role you are targeting and save the job description.
  • List your education, roles, dates and relevant skills accurately.
  • Describe achievements with evidence you can explain.
  • Include a professional contact method and check spelling.
  • Avoid sharing identity documents or unnecessary personal information.
Get help with your next step ↗
CHECKLIST / 03

Get ready for an application

  • Start with the provider’s official website and current instructions.
  • Confirm eligibility and the exact deadline, including its time zone.
  • Make a checklist of the required documents and file formats.
  • Check that names and dates match across your documents.
  • Keep login credentials private and retain your submission receipt.
