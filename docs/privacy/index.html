YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy, simply.

This website does not use an enquiry database or advertising cookies.

Enquiries

The contact form prepares a message in your browser. It does not submit your details to a Makers website server, put them into a link, or save them in browser storage. You can review and copy the message, then open WhatsApp and paste it. Copying places the text on your device clipboard. WhatsApp’s privacy policy and terms apply when you use that service, and you choose whether to send.

External services

Images, fonts, styles and scripts are hosted with this website; loading a page does not request assets from Google Fonts or Unsplash. GitHub Pages may process technical access information to deliver and secure the site. Opening WhatsApp takes you to an external service.

What to share

Only share the information needed for your enquiry. Do not send passwords, one-time codes or sensitive identity documents in your first message.

Questions

For questions about information you have shared with Makers, call 054 139 6869.
