YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy, simply.

This website does not use an enquiry database or advertising cookies.

Enquiries

The contact form builds a WhatsApp message in your browser. Details are not submitted to a Makers website server. When you continue to WhatsApp, its privacy policy and terms apply. You decide whether to send the message.

External services

The website uses a photograph hosted by Unsplash. Loading it shares standard connection information, such as your IP address, with that service. The hosting provider may also process basic technical access logs to deliver and secure the site.

What to share

Only share the information needed for your enquiry. Do not send passwords, one-time codes or sensitive identity documents in your first message.

Questions
