MEET OUR FOUNDER

Emmanuel
Larbi Kwesi

Founder, Makers Educational Consult

Excellence. Integrity. Service.

Emmanuel Larbi Kwesi is an education enthusiast, researcher, and emerging leader with a strong passion for educational advancement, youth empowerment, and international development. He is the founder of Makers Educational Consult, an educational consultancy established with the vision of making quality educational opportunities more accessible and helping individuals make informed decisions about their academic and professional futures.

As a student at International Christian University (ICU) in Japan, Emmanuel brings an international perspective to educational development, informed by his exposure to diverse academic environments, cultures, and institutional practices.

Beyond his academic pursuits, Emmanuel serves as President of the ICU Africana Circle, where he promotes intercultural understanding, student engagement, leadership development, and collaboration between African students and the wider international community. His involvement in educational and cultural exchange initiatives reflects his commitment to building meaningful connections and creating opportunities for young people.

Through Makers Educational Consult, Emmanuel envisions an organisation that bridges the gap between ambition and opportunity by providing reliable educational guidance, encouraging academic excellence, and empowering students to pursue their aspirations with confidence.

His leadership philosophy is grounded in the belief that education is more than the acquisition of knowledge; it is a powerful instrument for personal transformation, opportunity, and sustainable societal development.

Driven by a commitment to excellence, integrity, and service, Emmanuel aspires to position Makers Educational Consult as a trusted partner in educational advancement and human capital development.
