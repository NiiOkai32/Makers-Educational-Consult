A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

How do I get started?+

Call 054 139 6869 or message us on WhatsApp with the service you need, a short description of your goal and your deadline.

How much do your services cost?+

Pricing depends on the service and agreed scope. Contact Makers to discuss your requirements and receive a quote before work begins.

Can you help with my thesis or project work?+

We offer guidance, planning, feedback, presentation support and data analysis assistance. Your submitted academic work should remain your own and follow your institution’s rules.

Do you guarantee a scholarship or application approval?+

No. We assist with preparation and registration. Scholarship providers, passport authorities and other official bodies make their own decisions.
